Artist statement

My working approach to art is primarily conceptual, and I'm

currently expanding my practice from installation, and text-based

into new mediums such as video, and performance. I think of art as

a different form of communication, one that can bring people

together, or create space to reflect differently on ideas and

conventions. My work usually grows out of philosophy, other artists'

work, and conversations, and it's this last part that has increasingly

drawn me toward working in a group rather than alone.
